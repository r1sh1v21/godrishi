God Rishi is a digital chronicle of essays, philosophical meditations, and observations at the intersection of technology, culture, and solitude.

The design adheres to the raw, stripped-back aesthetics of the early web: pure darkness, crisp typography, and unadorned prose.

No frameworks. No analytics. No noise.
