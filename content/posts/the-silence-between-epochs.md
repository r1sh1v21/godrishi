When an epoch comes to its conclusion, it rarely does so with a fanfare. The systems and assumptions that defined a generation slowly hollow out from within, until only an external shell remains.

Those who observe closely can sense the threshold long before the collapse becomes public fact.

### The Sacredness of the Void

The transition periods between civilizational modes are characterized by profound ambiguity. Old vocabularies fail to describe emerging realities, while new idioms have yet to solidify.

In this interregnum, the most radical act is not frantic reaction, but rigorous quietude.
