In the early centuries of manuscript illumination, cloistered monks worked by candlelight, transcribing sacred codices with painstaking reverence. Each letter was a deliberate stroke against the entropy of time.

In our current era, the digital monastery exists in reverse: rather than preserving rare knowledge against oblivion, we must preserve human focus against an overwhelming flood of triviality.

### Ascetic Computing

What does ascetic computing look like in practice?

- **Minimalist interfaces:** Clean black screens, raw hypertext, sans frameworks.
- **Local sovereignty:** Plain text files that will remain readable fifty years from now.
- **Unbroken attention:** The refusal to let algorithmic feeds dictate the tempo of conscious thought.

`
+------------------------------------+
|  Input -> Reflection -> Synthesis  |
+------------------------------------+
`

We do not need more tools; we need greater discernment in the tools we permit to cross the threshold of our attention.
